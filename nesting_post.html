The Russian Nesting Post

A quote post sits inside the post it quotes. Chain those together and you get a tower — and the interesting towers are the ones where every quote out-likes the thing it quotes, so the doll gets bigger with every layer. The bottom has to be a real original post, not another quote. Across 17,511,260 quote links in this snapshot, 1,969,103 of them increasing, here are the extremes: the tower that goes deepest, the one carrying the most likes, and — dropping the like rule entirely — the deepest quote stack on the site.

the tallest tower

5 deep, and it never once goes down

5 posts, and the like count goes up at every single step — 100 likes at the original, 1,963 at the outermost quote. 4 of the 5 are one account continuing its own thread, and the rest are other people picking it up. This is the deepest strictly-increasing quote chain in the snapshot that bottoms out on a genuine original post rather than on somebody else's quote — no chain of 6 exists.

ignoring likes entirely

The deepest quote stack on the site is 2,411 posts

Drop the like rule and just ask how deep quote-of-a-quote goes. The answer is not a viral cascade at all — it is two people writing a story to each other. Every post quotes the one before it, strictly alternating between the two accounts on all 2,410 handoffs, for 52d 17h straight. Engagement is beside the point: the most liked post in the entire chain got 8 likes, and 1,202 of the 2,411 got none at all. They are using quote posts as a threading mechanism, and it builds by far the deepest structure in the snapshot.

method

How this was computed

Every row of posts with a non-null quote_uri_id becomes an edge child → the post it quotes, joined to post_aggs for the like count on both ends: 17,511,260 edges over 24,343,275 posts. Keep only the edges where the quoting post has strictly more likes (1,969,103 of them). Because likes then strictly increase along every surviving edge the graph is acyclic, and sorting edges by the quoting post's like count is a topological order — so one linear pass gives both the longest path and the largest-sum path at each height. A chain may only start at a post whose own quote_uri_id is NULL and whose source is record: a genuine original we hold the body for. The deepest-stack section drops the like rule and walks the raw quote graph, which is a forest — each post quotes at most one post — so depth is a memoised pointer walk.

Caveats. Like counts are the snapshot's, over a 90-day activity window — a like cast outside it is not counted, so a tower spanning the window edge can be understated. target_only posts are never treated as roots: we saw them only as somebody else's quote target, so a NULL quote_uri_id there means "unobserved", not "not a quote". The snapshot stores structure, not content, so post text and handles were hydrated live from the public Bluesky appview at render time and may have moved since; like counts shown are the snapshot's, not live. Ties form no edge, so equal like counts never extend a tower.
